About InAmigos Foundation

InAmigos Foundation is a registered non-profit organization dedicated to bringing meaningful social change through education, environmental sustainability, youth development, animal welfare and community empowerment. The organization believes that positive change begins when people come together with compassion and purpose. By connecting volunteers, students, professionals and communities, InAmigos Foundation has successfully created opportunities that transform lives across India.

Our Ongoing Projects

Every initiative at InAmigos Foundation is designed to create meaningful and sustainable change in society.

Project Bachpanshala

Providing free education, mentorship and learning opportunities to underprivileged children, helping them build a brighter future.

Project Prakriti

Focused on environmental protection through plantation drives, cleanliness campaigns and sustainability awareness.

Project Jeev

Working for the welfare of stray animals through rescue initiatives, awareness campaigns and community participation.

Project Udaan

Empowering youth through skill development, internships, career guidance and personality development programs.

Project Seva

Organizing food distribution, clothing drives, blood donation camps and social welfare activities for communities in need.

Project Vikas

Supporting community development through awareness programs, leadership initiatives and opportunities for social growth.
